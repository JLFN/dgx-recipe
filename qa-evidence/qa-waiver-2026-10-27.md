# QA record: MiniMax-H3 unit (gate waived by the operator)

**Unit:** `e32a405` and `40296aa` on `feature/minimax-h3` in this repository:
the MiniMax-H3 contract, the launcher `scripts/start-minimax-h3.sh`, the
runbook and the README section, plus the two follow-up skill commits in the

**Date:** 2026-10-27.

**The rule 19 gate is waived for this unit.** Asked which model should review it
before the push, the operator answered "waive the gate and push as it stands".
The waiver is recorded here rather than left silent, and this project has no
recorded QA guardrail of its own, so there was no earlier model to fall back on.

`qa-evidence/qa-report.md` still holds the 2026-10-01 Bonsai report and is
deliberately not overwritten: no independent tester reviewed this unit, and
writing a report that ended in "verdict: overall PASS" would be a fabricated
verdict. What follows is the author's own evidence, which is what the operator
accepted in place of a QA pass. Read it as unverified by a second party.

## What the author checked, on the reference box (gx10-e1d2, 192.168.1.91)

Every line below was produced in this session and is quoted from the terminal
that ran it.

1. Syntax and the script's own contract: `bash -n` passes, and the launcher's
   `selftest` reports ok for the help path, the unknown-option path, the
   five-field body, the refusal codes, the bind parsing and the digest helper.
2. A sparse fixture at `/tmp/h3fix` exercises the three verify outcomes: a
   matching recorded digest, a mismatching one (reported and exit 1), and a
   Ref2VA component that differs from its FL2VA twin (reported). The
   incomplete-checkpoint path prints the byte-total mismatch and refuses start.
3. The launcher was transferred to the box over an HTTP fetch from this
   workstation and its sha256 matched on both sides
   (`8940dfbe…`, then `058a7e70…`, then `1c9fecba…`, then `9ed717aa…` as the
   three defects below were fixed).
4. `preflight` against the real checkpoint: both required files, four
   inventoried FL2VA components, the Ref2VA index, FL2VA 144,051,182,625 and
   Ref2VA 144,051,182,613 bytes, equal to the baselines the launcher carries;
   ffmpeg 6.1.1, nvcc 13.0, GB10 driver 580.178.04, port free, 122,147 MB
   available.
5. `build`: `make CUDA_ARCH=sm_121 h3-server-rs` produced
   `~/ds4-dfm-rs/h3-server-rs`, 5,084,200 bytes. The engine's `cuda-spark`
   target does not build it, which is why the launcher names the target.
6. `start --daemon` on 0.0.0.0:8090, ready in 2 s, listener confirmed on
   0.0.0.0:8090, process 213,072 KiB resident, engine banner reading
   `engine /mnt/h3-artifacts/MiniMax-H3 (steps 20, reuse 1)`.
7. `test` asserted the five refusals: 404 video_not_found, 400 invalid_task,
   400 unknown_field, 400 unsupported_short_edge, 404 unknown_url.
8. `verify --quick` matched both partition byte totals.
9. `stop` left no process and the port free; the checkpoint was only read.
10. The open-grok client reached the daemon from another host and printed the
    server's own `invalid_aspect_ratio` refusal for a 21:9 request.
11. The full client flow was exercised at no GPU cost against the engine's
    stand-in renderer on 0.0.0.0:8091: create, in_progress, completed, a
    177,349-byte download and an ffprobe; two jobs back to back showed `queued`
    with `queue_position` 1 behind an `in_progress` job, and the second job's
    content downloaded as 629,407 bytes. That host was stopped afterwards.
12. Three defects found on the box were fixed before the unit commit: the port
    check matched the wrong `ss` column, the verification-record check tested a
    helper's output instead of its exit status, and the `--renderer` path built
    its own command line.

- No second reader: an independent tester did not review the launcher, the
